Meet the Organizers

Rules of Villainy is a community focused unofficial Disney Lorcana organization that focuses on bringing entertaining tournaments to the St Louis area, and producing high quality recorded and live content. This event is made possible by our partnership with Impact Gaming Center in Fairview Heights, Illinois. Our goal is to share our love of the Disney Lorcana Trading Card Game with as many people as possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

If you cannot find the answer to your question below, please connect with us here.

Are there Attendee or Guest tickets?

Currently we will not be offering nor requiring attendee or guest tickets for entry to the event. Friends and family are welcome to join, however please note that if space becomes limited, competitors will receieve priority over non-paying guests.

How can I submit my deck list?

Decklist submission will be done through the Ravensburger Playhub. Links to each event are available in the details section of the corresponding tournament here.

What is the refund policy?

Refunds are handled through Impact Gaming Center. Please call the store to determine if a refund is available.

How can ADA or other special accomadations be requested?

Please reach out to us via the contact details here with information on your request.

Can I volunteer to help with the event?

We appreciate your support and while we do not have an official process for accepting volunteers, please reach out to us via the contact details here with information on how you would like to assist.

How can I sponsor the event?

We do not have an official sponsorship application available at this time, however you can always reach out to us via the contact details here.

Where can I get more details on Hotel/Accomodations?

A Hotel block is available for attendees. Basic details are available here. Additional details can be obtained by contacting the hotel directly.
